Advanced
Waterproofing Systems

In NY and NJ, water is the primary enemy of structural longevity. We deploy multi-layer defensive systems that ensure your foundation remains bone-dry for decades.

Exterior Membranes

Self-adhering, high-density polymer sheets that create an unbreakable barrier between soil moisture and your concrete.

Crystalline Injection

Chemical solutions that penetrate deep into concrete pores, reacting with water to form non-soluble crystals.

French Drains & Sump

Sub-surface drainage systems designed to redirect hydrostatic pressure away from the footing and foundation walls.
